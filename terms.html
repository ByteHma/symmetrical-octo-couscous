Terms of Use

Intended use and limits of this personal utility.

Effective: October 14, 2026 · Operator: ByteHma

1. Personal use only

This project documents an OAuth connection used by its owner to access Google Drive through a local rclone installation for personal backup and recovery. The public website is informational and does not offer a hosted backup product, registrations, or accounts to third parties.

2. Authorized access

The connector is intended for Google Drive data the account owner is authorized to access through the granted drive.file scope. It should not be used to access anyone else's data without permission.

3. Responsibility for backups

The owner is responsible for configuring local backup and encryption tools, securing credentials, verifying transfers, maintaining recovery information, and testing restores. The public website does not perform any of these operations.

4. Availability and changes

Functionality depends on local software, network availability, Google Drive, permissions, and applicable service policies. The owner may change or discontinue this personal configuration. No public service level or availability guarantee is offered.

5. Third-party services

Google Drive, GitHub Pages, and rclone are separate services or tools governed by their own terms and policies. This independent project is not endorsed by or affiliated with Google or rclone.

6. Privacy and contact

See the Privacy Policy for information on Google user data and website visitors. Questions can be directed through the maintainer's GitHub profile; do not post private data publicly.
